# CSM compatibility: the reconnect-hang class and two solution paths (issue [#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107) lineage)

[中文](csm-compat.md) | [English](csm-compat.en.md) · [Credits](credits.md) · [README](../README.en.md)

> 2026-11-02, branch `issue107-csm-compat`. Origin: [#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107) (AD103 4080S / ASUS B550-F) showed CSM on → hang at `RECONNECT: ConnectController (recursive=true) begin`, CSM off → whole chain green (+82–88%); the unlock writes and the FLR survival were identical in both modes — the fault sits purely in reconnect. This document is the signature table for the CSM class, the implemented explicit native-GOP recovery path, the experiment matrix and the evidence index. (The evaluated Driver####/bcfg pre-registration form was dropped: dynamic loading already covers the need, so no permanent NVRAM entry is required.)

## 0. Who needs CSM — the audience of this compatibility patch (read this first)

**We still strongly recommend that the vast majority of users turn CSM off and run pure UEFI** — every default path, performance validation and display-stability result in this repository is based on CSM off. CSM off removes this whole family of symptoms in one move (reconnect hang, Code 12 after handoff, display-routing drift); there is no reason to turn it on for this tool.

But CSM on has a set of **real users who cannot simply drop it**; on their machines CSM is not optional:

1. **Old devices that depend on legacy-only OPROMs**: some old RAID cards, capture cards and professional cards carry only a legacy x86 ROM, and under pure UEFI the firmware never executes their OPROM, so the device is unusable pre-boot (RAID-card boot volumes, old-card POST screens, etc.);
2. **Companion display cards with no UEFI GOP**: value-minded users running modded CMP mining cards next to an old display card (GT 210 / early Quadro NVS / pre-GT 610 generations / AMD HD7XXX and older) — their VBIOS has **no EFI driver to extract**, so this tool's native-GOP compatibility path, ReBAR and chaining all have nothing to work with, and under pure UEFI such cards may not even light up;
3. **VBIOS too small to hold a GOP**: some old cards have a 64 KB VBIOS that is already full, and a UEFI GOP module (~130 KB before compression) physically does not fit — "just flash a VBIOS with a GOP" is not an option for them;
4. Systems booting from a legacy disk / old enterprise platforms (some OEMs hard-lock CSM behavior).

These users **need the unlock just as much** (mining and old cards are exactly where the clamps hit hardest), and their CSM-on environments are precisely the exposure surface of the reconnect-hang family ([#106](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/106)/[#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107)/[#133](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/133)/[#134](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/134)/[#157](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/157)) — so this compatibility patch is not a convenience for people who "could not be bothered to turn CSM off"; it is for machines that **have no switch**. If you are unsure whether you belong to any of the cases above: **try CSM off first, and once it works do not turn it back on**.

**Since 2026-11-04 native-GOP is the default behavior** (maintainer ruling; four bench rounds on the local Z170 plus field records from two machines, [#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107)/[#128](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/128); **the method was contributed by the #107 reporter [seti9585](https://github.com/seti9585)**, see [credits](credits.md)): rebuilding the console from the card's own stock GOP in its shadowed ROM replaces the firmware `ConnectController` reconnect. Design axiom = **a wrong reconnect is worse than none — however the console came up, that is how it continues**: the stock driver is rebuilt from the stock ROM, any failure (extraction / decompression / binding / child verification) leaves the boot-time state untouched and lets bootmgfw self-establish, and it **never falls back to the ConnectController that hangs**. Risk asymmetry argument: on a CSM board the old default's worst case is a hard hang that forces a power cycle, while native-GOP's worst case is a frozen frame until Windows takes over (all four rounds reached Windows); the only known failure shape is "child installation is non-deterministic under mixed display wiring" (caused by the CSM module grabbing the console; two rounds ended differently), and the graceful fall-through catches both ends. Opt-out valve = `\tx-reconnect-legacy.txt` at the stick root (restores the old recursive + PR#13 logic); `\tx-native-gop.txt` remains as an optional GOP-module pin. If the failure shape **hang inside StartImage** (not a verify failure) ever shows up, the defaulting gets reverted — that is the only failure surface we worry about.

## 1. Pathology signature

| Observation point | CSM on | CSM off |
|---|---|---|
| Selector writes / FLR survival | normal | normal |
| After `RECONNECT: ConnectController begin` | no further lines (hang inside the firmware ConnectController; suspicion = re-selecting the already-stopped BiosVideo binding) | `ok (recursive=true)` |
| Log blind spot (raised by the [#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107) reporter) | v2.1.8 flushed the RECONNECT line before entering the console mirror, so a hang inside ConnectController could not be told apart from a hung console output, nor could one see which display driver got selected | — |

**Blind-spot fix since v2.1.9**: the `DIAG *-OPENERS` lines now carry driver-image base+size fingerprints (`tx_detach.rs::log_openers`), so both before detach and after reconnect one can see whether BiosVideo or a vendor GOP owns the GPU.

**Three further failure shapes the same rebuild retires** (field records; not part of this document's matrix). All share the mechanism: the forced `DisconnectController`/`ConnectController` returns with the display engine left in its post-FLR state and no link training or mode set, so the OS inherits a half-dead pipeline. (a) **Multi-head / specific outputs**: after an unlock, a black screen during boot with a broken Windows session behind it on particular connectors or on more than one display — [#105](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/105), closed by the rebuild on the same layouts (three heads, single head on a non-primary port, hot-plug). (b) **Laptop internal panel brightness (DDC-CI)**: the backlight dropped out and brightness control stopped responding after an unlock on some Legion SKUs (one R7000 2024 confirmed, fixed in v2.1.10; the backlight/DDC-CI channel rides the internal link the reconnect leaves untrained). (c) **Handoff BSOD + virtual display**: Z370 + 2080 SUPER (TU104 `1e81`, iGPU on, single dGPU head, Win10 GSP) showed a handoff black screen and an `APC_INDEX_MISMATCH` BSOD loop starting ~42 s into Windows in the v2.1.9 era ([#55](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/55), filed back then under the "handoff integrity" #35/#61 lineage) — maintainer self-tested on the same machine with the new build (native-GOP default): both shapes gone, and **the virtual display (software virtual display adapter) form on this machine was also retired by the same rebuild**; the report's other dangling reading (relock `0xffffffef`) was confirmed known-good by #74/#191. The card's own GOP driver performs the full initialization, link training included, which is why the rebuild closes all four. Scoped accordingly: opting back into `\tx-reconnect-legacy.txt` is expected to bring all three shapes back.

## 2. Path one: explicit native-GOP recovery (`\tx-native-gop.txt`, tx builds)

**Explicitly select** the stock GOP driver before reconnecting instead of calling the generic ConnectController that hangs ("ConnectController priority is not an exclusion filter" — a preference list still does not exclude the other candidates; you must Start explicitly):

1. Obtain the GOP EFI image: the first line of `\tx-native-gop.txt` is a module filename on the volume (e.g. `\gop.efirom`, from the make-usbroot compatibility pack or regenerated locally from this card's own VBIOS dump via `assets extract-gop`); an empty/absent first line = use this card's PciBus shadow ROM (`PciIo RomImage`).
2. `romcore` parsing (PCIR chain + NPDE stepping → UEFI 13.4.2 EFI ROM header → PE32+ validation); the compressed payload (CompressionType=1, as for every NVIDIA GOP) goes to the **firmware's own EFI_DECOMPRESS_PROTOCOL** — that is also what PciBus uses for compressed OPROMs, the authoritative implementation, and the Rust side stays pure parsing.
3. `LoadImage(FromBuffer)` + `StartImage`; locate **exactly one** Driver Binding belonging to the new image (`18A031AB-…`) and run explicit `Supported()`→`Start()` on the target card.
4. Readback verification (constitution §1.1): a GOP child belonging to the target card must appear (node-aligned `path_belongs_to` check).
5. Console recovery runs `ConnectController(child, non-recursive)` **on the GOP child only** — a child-level connect never touches PCI-level drivers, so BiosVideo cannot get re-selected.
6. VGA forwarding (the `0x001C` subset captured pre-claim) + IO|MEM decoding are restored via PciIo Attributes; **BME stays off** (the handoff shape is unchanged).

Failure policy: any step failing → log it and hand the console to bootmgfw to self-establish (the proven safe landing point), **never falling back to the hanging ConnectController**.

## 3.1 Field records (updated 2026-11-03)

- **CSM Disabled as the single-variable elimination of C1 already has three cases**: [#107](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/107) (B550/AD103, original report), [#133](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/133) (B350M/2206, closed at +90%), with [#134](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/134) on the same board family pending. **CSM off is the first action for C1** (before any marker experiment).
- **Reference evidence shape for "CSM occupying legacy VGA"** (observed in [#133](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/133)): with CSM on, `pnputil /enum-devices /resources` contains IO `3B0-3BB`/`3C0-3DF` + MEM `A0000-BFFFF`; after CSM off these disappear while the rest is unchanged.
- **Black-screen misread warning** (hit by the [#133](https://github.com/physicsdolphin/nv-uefi-unlocker/issues/133) reporter): after clearing all markers the loader waits 30 s at CONFIRM and then prints `ABORT (zero GPU writes)` — easy to misread on a black screen as "CSM made no difference". Verdict point: an ABORT last line = the transaction never ran, not a transaction failure.
- An empty marker (0 bytes) has been a legal switch form since the v2.1.9 asset rebuild (`fc1f70ac` onwards); no placeholder byte needed.

## 3.2 Local TU106 re-verification (2026-11-04, Z170 + 2070, first native-GOP Turing bench round)

The transaction was fully green (unlock_persist, FLR skipped as designed), the HANDOFF-OUT family tag debuted correctly (`tu10x:unlock=true`), and after the native-GOP chain reached **Start=SUCCESS** one driver-level fact surfaced:

- **Whether the TU1xx GOP driver installs a child is not deterministic** (four-round correction): under the same "monitor on the iGPU" topology, one round showed after 750 ms of three probes only the iGPU's GOP child (probe hex decodes to `Acpi(PNP0A03,0)/Pci(0x2,0)/AcpiAdr`, exactly the child shape already known from PR13), while **another round showed a dGPU child at probe 0 (36 B = dGPU path + AcpiAdr) → `verified GOP child (3840x2160)` → `console rebound` → `complete (display path verified)`** — the first end-to-end complete success of native-GOP on Turing (evidence `evidence/tu106-native-gop-z170/loader-native-gop-success.log`).
- **The fourth round (pure-UEFI control) pinned the variable precisely: who holds the boot console decides whether the child gets installed** (`loader-pure-uefi-success.log`). Back on pure UEFI the console ownership inverts — the only GOP-PROBE handle is **the dGPU's** (36 B, `on_target=true`), the dGPU enters as **D0 active instead of D3cold** (SAMPLE fully readable, NV_PROM crc normal; in the CSM-on round the dGPU sat parked by RTD3 with SAMPLE all-ones), `pre-claim attributes raw=0x300` (0x0 in the CSM-on round — the CSM module even disturbs the PciIo attributes tracking), and detach releases cleanly (GOP 6→4→no handles). native-GOP **hits at probe 0 with 4K** → console rebound → the dGPU is visible throughout Windows with no fake-freeze segment. Conclusion: **under CSM on the CSM module grabs the boot console for the iGPU, which is the root of the dGPU child-installation uncertainty**; pure UEFI is deterministic. native-GOP also works well under pure UEFI (its actual job there = seamlessly rebuilding the detached dGPU console, without waiting for bootmgfw to rebind) — the marker is not CSM-exclusive. Side observation: the 0x823xxx FEAT alias mirror was readable in the CSM-on round but all-badf in the pure-UEFI round (0x4096xx always readable) — alias decode difference to investigate, does not affect the transaction.
- **Localization converges (both outcomes of the mixed shape are now recorded)**: when **the monitor is on the dGPU but CSM routes the boot console to the iGPU** (the local shape), a successful native-GOP leaves the physical monitor on the dGPU port **frozen at the rebind frame** — bootmgfw paints the iGPU console, nobody updates the dGPU port — until the Windows dGPU driver takes over and lights the lock screen (no Code 12). Visually there is a "fake-freeze" stretch, which is the expected path rather than a fault; while in the genuine "monitor on the iGPU" shape a failed native-GOP → bootmgfw uses the iGPU console and the iGPU port **displays normally without freezing**. Neither outcome blocks Windows. The essence of the frozen frame = between the rebind frame and OS takeover the boot console is separated from the physical output port (CSM display routing); under pure UEFI console and physical port agree, so the segment does not exist.
- **Two hibernation-resume shapes**: on a successful resume (hiberfile accepted) drivers/resources are intact, no Code 12, the dGPU just works — only a cold-start Windows resource re-arbitration hits the arbitration failure, which narrows Code 12 to a **pure arbitration problem**; on a failed resume Windows detects the GPU state does not match the hiberfile, **discards the image and boots normally** (observed in the fourth round), no loss, unlock intact. Hibernation + unlock remains a sensitive combination; plain shutdown/restart is recommended day to day.
- Disabling→enabling the device in Device Manager does not drop the unlock (in-OS self-recovery), see the TU106 section of compatibility.md.
- The Z170's NVRAM Windows option is a **mixed HD short-form + FILE node shape** (`hd(partN,sig) + file:"\EFI\Microsoft\Boot\BOOTMGFW.EFI"`); short-form resolve matches on partition identity per BmExpandPartitionDevicePath — three for three on hardware (the second platform validating the diag.5 fields).

## 4. Where GOP modules come from (no download dependency, per constitution §1.6)

1. **This card's shadow ROM (default, zero downloads)**: during enumeration the firmware has already copied the whole option ROM into UEFI RAM (`PciIo RomImage`); the loader parses it directly — an absolute match with the card and no distribution problem.
2. **The user's own VBIOS dump**: dump the card's ROM with GPU-Z or similar → `assets extract-gop <dump> <outdir>` extracts the `.efirom` module locally (SHA-256 printed and pinned).
3. **Compatibility pack (make-usbroot wiring, planned)**: the GOP driver exists only inside the VBIOS ROM (NVIDIA driver packages do not contain it); the pinned object is the official stock VBIOS dump per family (archived e.g. in the TechPowerUp VBIOS database, SHA-256 into the manifest), downloaded by the script and extracted **locally** — isomorphic to how gsp.bin/booter ship, and the project never self-hosts NVIDIA firmware. The compressed .efirom is used as-is: decompression happens at runtime through the firmware EFI_DECOMPRESS_PROTOCOL, with zero decompression code on the loader side.

## 5. Open items

1. **Decompressing to PE in assets**: not needed for now — compatibility-pack modules are compressed .efirom and decompression happens at runtime via the firmware EFI_DECOMPRESS_PROTOCOL, with zero decompression code on the loader side. If a "PE on disk" form is ever needed, consider host-side Tiano decoding then (a cross-check repro is retained and can be compared line by line against EDK2 BaseUefiDecompressLib semantics).
2. The rewrite semantics of the trailing VID/DID + checksum in GOP modules (the normalization logic of Wudimobile GOP Updater) are most likely irrelevant to **runtime LoadImage** (Supported() looks at the PCI device, not the tail); confirm while doing field verification; only the GOP-flashing replacement scenario needs them.
